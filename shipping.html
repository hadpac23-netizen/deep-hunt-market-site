DESTINATION-AWARE SHIPPING

Shipping is verified at checkout.

HUNT does not pretend one shipping price or delivery time applies worldwide. Supplier, variant, stock, destination and method are rechecked before payment.

Before payment

For sellable products, checkout must recheck the selected variant, current stock and destination shipping. If a verified shipping method is unavailable, the order should not proceed.

Delivery estimates

Any delivery window is an estimate unless checkout expressly states a guaranteed date. Cross-border shipments may be affected by carrier operations, customs, local holidays and events outside reasonable control. Mandatory delivery-time rights in the customer's jurisdiction remain unaffected.

Taxes, duties and import charges

Destination taxes, duties or import charges must be disclosed or handled as required by law. HUNT should not describe a pre-tax estimate as the final total where mandatory charges remain unknown.

Tracking

When real-money ordering is activated, HUNT intends to show order status and supplier/carrier tracking when available. The launch gate remains blocked until order creation and tracking transitions are proven end-to-end.

Wrong address, failed delivery and lost parcels

Customers should verify their delivery details before payment. Failed-delivery, lost-parcel and re-shipment handling will follow carrier evidence, supplier terms and mandatory consumer rights. Final customer-support routing must be configured before real-money launch.
