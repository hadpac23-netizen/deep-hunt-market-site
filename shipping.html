1. Where HUNT ships

Shipping availability is determined per item and destination. A country appearing in a selector does not by itself guarantee that every item can ship there. HUNT verifies route availability before an order can be accepted.

2. Shipping price and estimates

Shipping price and delivery estimates are item-, warehouse- and destination-specific. HUNT will display or confirm the verified information available for the order before acceptance. We do not publish a universal delivery promise that is not supported by the active route.

3. Processing and transit

Order processing time and carrier transit time are separate. Processing may include payment confirmation, exact-variant inventory recheck and supplier/warehouse handoff. Tracking is provided when the carrier or fulfillment route supplies it.

4. Split shipments

An order containing multiple items may arrive in more than one shipment. Where that occurs, available tracking information may be provided separately for each parcel.

5. Taxes, duties and customs

Destination taxes, duties and import handling depend on the route and market. HUNT will not label an estimate as final tax truth unless it has been verified for the checkout route. If a required tax or customs treatment cannot be verified, the affected purchase remains blocked from real-money checkout.

6. Address changes

Contact Support as soon as possible if an address is wrong. Once a parcel has been submitted to fulfillment or a carrier, an address change may no longer be possible. Mandatory consumer rights are not affected by this operational limitation.

7. Delayed, lost or damaged parcels

If tracking stalls, a parcel is reported lost, or a delivery arrives damaged, contact hacoachle@gmail.com. HUNT will review the order and carrier/fulfillment evidence and apply the remedy required by the order terms and applicable law.

8. Pre-launch notice

Supplier Live Order is currently disabled. The shipping verification features shown on HUNT are being used for pre-launch testing and do not create a supplier order.

These policies are intended to operate alongside mandatory consumer and privacy law. Where applicable law grants stronger rights, those rights prevail. Before real-money checkout is enabled, HUNT will publish the additional transaction disclosures required for the active market, including the registered business address where required.
