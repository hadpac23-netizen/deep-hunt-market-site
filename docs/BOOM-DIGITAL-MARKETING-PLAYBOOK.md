# BOOM Digital Marketing Playbook

## Current 2026 lessons BOOM has adopted

### Google
Strong conversion/value data and strong creative inputs are prerequisites for AI campaign optimization. Campaign automation cannot compensate for poor measurement or weak product pages.

HUNT action:
- define a conversion ladder
- separate micro-conversions from verified purchase
- improve listing/page quality before paid acquisition

### TikTok Smart+
Automation can cover targeting, optimization and creative while still exposing advertiser controls.

HUNT action:
- design BOOM paid-media automation as modules
- owner controls paid launch and budget
- use catalog/product-native creative only

### Pinterest
Feed quality, site quality and signals should be fixed before bids and budgets.
Creative should be mobile-first, product-forward and useful/inspiring.

HUNT action:
- prioritize feed/site/performance hardening
- prepare 2:3 and 9:16 product-native templates

### Amazon Ads
Detail-page quality matters. Product/category targeting and negative targeting can reduce irrelevant placements.

HUNT action:
- Listing Readiness remains a promotion gate
- build a negative-intent taxonomy
- promote only products with strong data completeness

### Shopify
Marketing measurement benefits from viewing more than one attribution model.

HUNT action:
- prepare first-touch, last non-direct and assisted-journey reporting
- do not credit one channel blindly

### YouTube Shopping
Eligible stores/creators can connect stores and tag products in videos, Shorts and live content.

HUNT action:
- prepare eligibility and verified-product-media requirements
- activate only when HUNT/merchant commerce is eligible

## BOOM priority order
1. Measurement truth
2. Product/feed quality
3. Site speed
4. Organic SEO
5. Product-native creative
6. Personalization
7. Attribution
8. Partner/merchant growth
9. Paid-media readiness
10. Paid experiments only after owner approval

## Core rule
Marketing cannot be used to hide product, fulfillment or trust problems. BOOM fixes the foundation first, then scales what proves value.

## September 2026 operating update

### Learning before scaling
BOOM should treat every channel as a learning system, not a switch to spend money.
1. Validate product/feed quality.
2. Validate event and conversion tracking.
3. Run zero-spend onsite or organic experiments first.
4. Keep a baseline/control.
5. Promote only products whose page quality and fulfillment evidence pass.
6. Scale only when conversion value or contribution economics improve.

### Smart automation
- Search, likes and saves immediately update onsite merchandising signals.
- Product recommendations require real relevance; empty is better than random.
- Fragrance intent can move perfume/beauty collections upward automatically.
- Creative rotation should respond to measured fatigue, not arbitrary schedules.
- Paid automation stays locked until owner approval and measurement readiness.
