# HUNT HERO 4.2 — F35 MODERN AI EXPERIENCE MASTER PROMPT

## North Star
HUNT is a living global commerce world. It must feel cinematic, personal, multilingual and continuously discoverable while remaining fast, truthful and easy to shop.

## Brand
- Public consumer brand: HUNT only.
- Remove DEAL from the visible consumer wordmark.
- Give HUNT the previous DEAL accent treatment.
- HUNT wordmark uses a refined H → U → N → T cascade from above, then settles.
- Motion is subtle, premium and reduced-motion safe.
- A small curved accent gives the mark a warm, intelligent personality without looking childish.

## Hero World
Opening scene:
- Night mountains / stars.

Then rotate through:
- Dubai
- Hong Kong
- New York
- Paris
- Madrid
- London
- Singapore
- Seoul
- Shanghai
- Tokyo

Rules:
- Night only.
- No fashion model / woman-walking background.
- City or landscape first.
- One active video at a time.
- Current + next scene only.
- Videos use lightweight Pexels derivatives.
- Heavy/unavailable city videos may use a real night photo with subtle cinematic pan.
- Smooth crossfade.
- Manual next-scene control.
- Pause offscreen.
- Reduced motion uses still images.

## Country-aware scene priority
Mountains always opens first.
The next city is softly prioritized by destination market:
- MENA → Dubai
- HK → Hong Kong
- JP → Tokyo
- KR → Seoul
- SG/MY → Singapore
- CN/TW → Shanghai
- US/CA/MX → New York
- FR/BE/CH → Paris
- ES/PT → Madrid
- GB/IE → London
This affects presentation only, never catalog access or shipping claims.

## BOOM Lifestyle Stream
Replace a weak single promo with a queue of real catalog moments.
The stream may contain 8–12 stories derived from:
- explicit behavior signals
- category views
- product views
- likes
- saves
- cart
- searches
- fresh catalog evidence

Interest families:
- Women / fashion
- Beauty
- Accessories
- Tech
- Home
- Men
- Sports
- Kids

Rules:
- Never infer identity from name or appearance.
- Use behavior/preferences only.
- New users get a balanced discovery mix with fashion, beauty, accessories, home and tech.
- Existing users get their strongest interests first.
- Full catalog always remains available.

## Promo Island Motion
States:
IDLE → PEEK → OPEN → COLLAPSE → HIDE

Visual behavior:
- Compact lower-edge floating island.
- Stronger entrance using scale + translate + controlled glow.
- Small stack of next recommendations behind/near the primary card.
- One primary story at a time.
- Manual next/previous and mini-stack selection.
- Pause on hover/focus/touch.
- Dismiss remembered for session.
- Never cover navigation, search, cart or important imagery.
- No fake urgency, discount, popularity, reviews or stock.

## Multilingual Experience
Support:
- English
- Hebrew
- Arabic
- Spanish
- French
- Japanese
- Chinese

Brand remains HUNT.
Hero copy, search, category labels, promo helper copy, discovery prompts and product discovery cues localize.
RTL works for Hebrew and Arabic.
Language choice persists across Home → Category/Product → Checkout.

## Dynamic Scroll Discovery
Hero bottom cue rotates localized microcopy such as:
- Discover your HUNT
- More for you below
- Scroll into your world
The cue stays subtle, animated and reduced-motion safe.

## Product Page
Every product page communicates continuation:
- “Discover more for you ↓”
- Small dynamic Product Pulse appears after scrolling.
- Product Pulse can surface the first relevant related item when available.
- It never covers mobile navigation or Add to Cart.
- It disappears as the user reaches Endless Discovery.

## Performance
- No 4K background downloads.
- Use <= ~5MB video derivatives when possible.
- One active video.
- Images use optimized Pexels query params.
- Defer next media until needed.
- Transform/opacity motion only where possible.
- No layout shift.
- No unnecessary JS animation framework.
- Stop work when Hero leaves viewport.

## Accessibility
- prefers-reduced-motion
- keyboard controls
- visible focus
- meaningful aria labels
- dismiss controls
- no essential information only in animation
- RTL support

## Truth Rules
Never fabricate:
- discounts
- scarcity
- popularity
- purchase counts
- reviews
- stock
- shipping
- “NEW”
- price drops
- sponsored status

Internal HUNT recommendations are labeled as HUNT/BOOM lifestyle discovery, not sponsored advertising. Future paid placements must be explicitly labeled.
