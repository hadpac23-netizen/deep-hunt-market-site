# BOOM Marketing University

Completion means passing practical HUNT tests, not reading lessons.

## Degree 1 — Commerce Foundations
- consumer decision journey
- merchandising
- category architecture
- pricing/unit economics
- conversion funnels
- trust/reviews/returns
- search and discovery

Test: create a measurable category or product-page hypothesis.

## Degree 2 — Advertising
- positioning
- message strategy
- copywriting
- creative briefs
- media planning
- paid search
- product/Shopping ads
- social video
- measurement
- creative testing
- policy/brand safety

Test: produce a paused campaign plan with conversion value, creative matrix, landing page and stop rules.

## Degree 3 — Performance & Analytics
- CTR
- CVR
- AOV
- CAC
- LTV
- ROAS
- contribution margin
- cohorts
- incrementality
- attribution limits
- A/B testing
- bidding

Test: choose winners using profit, not vanity metrics.

## Degree 4 — SEO
- keyword research
- intent mapping
- product/category SEO
- technical SEO
- structured data
- internal linking
- backlinks/digital PR
- international SEO

Test: launch and audit one country/category SEO plan.

## Degree 5 — CRM & Retention
- consent
- lifecycle segmentation
- onboarding
- browse/cart recovery
- post-purchase
- win-back
- loyalty
- review/UGC loops
- frequency caps
- holdouts

## Degree 6 — Global Growth
- market sizing
- localization
- shipping economics
- payments
- returns
- tax/legal readiness
- local creative
- channel mix

Test: reject a high-demand market when fulfillment economics fail.

## Degree 7 — AI Merchandising & Personalization
- event taxonomy
- retrieval/ranking
- cold start
- exploration/exploitation
- diversity
- recommendation evaluation
- privacy controls
- causal experiments

Test: prove personalized ranking beats a baseline holdout.

## Core principle
BOOM stays behind the scenes. The shopper sees a simple store. Different users may see different ranking, modules, language, recommendations and offers, but every claim and price must remain truthful.
