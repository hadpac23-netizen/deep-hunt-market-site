## BOOM decision
A = quality evidence + media + commercial fit + fulfillment fit.
B = promising but missing proof or terms.
HOLD = not ready for integration.
REJECT = counterfeit risk, weak identity/QC, misleading media, poor reliability, or bad economics.

No supplier is considered “agreed” until they reply and the terms are verified.
