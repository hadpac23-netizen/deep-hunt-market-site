## Required reply fields
Every supplier is asked for:
- current catalog
- sample terms + MOQ
- wholesale tiers
- material/QC evidence
- high-resolution photos/video + written usage permission
- stock/feed/API/CSV options
- dropshipping/direct fulfillment
- delivery regions/times
- private label where relevant
- HUNT launch promotion / exclusive deal structure

## BOOM decision
A = quality evidence + media + commercial fit + fulfillment fit.
B = promising but missing proof or terms.
HOLD = not ready for integration.
REJECT = counterfeit risk, weak identity/QC, misleading media, poor reliability, or bad economics.

No supplier is considered “agreed” until they reply and the terms are verified.

## HUNT underwear + socks sourcing rules
- Scope: women, men, kids/teens, everyday basics, sleepwear and socks. Women's thong briefs may be sourced as a normal apparel category; no sexualized merchandising.
- Required variant fields: provider SKU/VID, exact provider size label, size system when supplied (EU/US/UK/alpha/age/cm), color, material, stock per variant, source timestamp.
- Never invent size conversion. If a provider does not supply an EU/US/UK conversion, HUNT shows the original provider size only.
- Shoes/apparel: choose color + size first, then verify stock for the selected provider variant before cart.
- Kids products require age/size data and destination-market safety/compliance evidence before private-label purchase.
- Required quote fields: 5-10 styles, samples, 100/300/500 tiers, MOQ by style/color, full color card/Pantone, size chart/grading, materials, QC, packaging/logo, lead time, shipping/DDP, media rights.
- Private Label remains preview-only until quality + landed cost + Profit Gate + fulfillment + payment terms pass. No inventory purchase now.
- Shelf suppliers need approved API/feed/media rights, live price and per-variant stock, shipping/returns data and a lawful checkout route.
- VogaCloset: do not scrape or reuse site media/content. Use only an approved affiliate/partner feed or written permission.
