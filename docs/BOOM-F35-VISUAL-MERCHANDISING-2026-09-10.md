# BOOM F35 — Visual Merchandising & Shelf Intelligence
Date: 2026-09-10

## Mission
Make HUNT feel curated, current and trustworthy before adding more catalog volume.

## Current trend signals — Fall 2026
Fresh editorial signals worth testing, not blindly copying:
- silk scarves
- floral brooches
- charm necklaces
- pearls
- wrap/shield sunglasses
- western belts
- drawstring/pouch bags
- animal-print bags
- brown denim
- suede jackets
- peplum jackets
- roll-neck knitwear
- satin ballet flats
- olive-toned bags

Phone-accessory signal:
- crossbody and wrist-strap carry systems for current iPhone
- magnetic/kickstand cases
- Qi2 / magnetic charging, but charger certification must be verified before claims

## What HUNT should show first
1. Women first.
2. BOOM F35 Trend Drop — 8–10 diverse current picks.
3. Department navigation.
4. Personalized For You.
5. Full live shelves.
6. Optional BOOM survey after products, not before them.
7. Provider/engineering status stays secondary.

## Product-card minimum
- strong image
- clear title
- real current/source price when known
- no fake discount
- category fit
- click target large enough for mobile
- compatibility visible for phone accessories

## Image rules
Fashion:
- hero/lifestyle image may lead
- show fabric/finish/detail images on product page

Functional accessories:
- full product framing; do not crop ports/buttons/case edges
- show compatibility and function clearly

Video supplements images; it does not replace a strong image set.

## Mobile course
Treat mobile as primary, not a reduced desktop.
- avoid duplicate list items for color variants
- categories must use shopper language
- provide useful filters for compatibility, price, quality/reviews, new arrivals and deals
- product galleries need clear additional-image cues
- support zoom/detail inspection
- keep shipping/returns/availability discoverable
- avoid hiding critical information in horizontal tab mazes

## BOOM trend-selection rules
A trend item does not enter because an editor mentioned it.
It must pass:
- current source
- category match
- image
- price
- seller/supplier quality threshold
- brand/IP review where required
- product-quality gate
- duplicate control

Trend Drop must stay diverse: max 2 items per trend and limited concentration per category.

## What we still do not know well enough
1. Which trend signals actually convert on HUNT.
2. Which hero-image style lowers bounce by category.
3. The ideal number of products above the fold on each viewport.
4. Supplier-media quality before samples arrive.
5. True return/defect rate by SKU.
6. Exact landed margin by destination.
7. API/feed reliability under sustained traffic and rate limits.
8. Which products remain available after 7/30/90 days.
9. Compatibility error rate for phone cases.
10. Whether videos improve conversion enough to justify page weight.
11. Which filters shoppers actually use.
12. How much personalization improves conversion vs a strong default shelf.

## Required learning loop
BASELINE -> ONE TEST -> CONVERSION KPI -> RETURN/AOV GUARDRAILS -> KEEP/KILL.

Never optimize only clicks. A high-click product with high returns is not a winner.
