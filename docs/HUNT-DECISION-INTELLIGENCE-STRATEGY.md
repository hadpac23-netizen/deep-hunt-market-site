# HUNT DEAL — Decision Intelligence Strategy

## Core position

HUNT DEAL is not another store, coupon site, or generic comparison page.
It is a shopping decision-intelligence system.

The shopper gives HUNT a mission:
- product or need
- budget
- size / variant / style
- delivery destination and deadline
- must-have conditions
- acceptable alternatives

BOOM then searches verified commerce sources and returns a decision rather than a wall of products.

## HUNT Mission

A mission can end in four states:
1. BUY NOW — evidence and value are strong.
2. WAIT — product fit is good but timing or value is weak.
3. SWITCH — another option fits the mission better.
4. NO-GO — evidence, stock, delivery, source or value is not good enough.

## Deal Passport

Every candidate gets a structured Deal Passport:
- exact product / variant match confidence
- source identity
- source freshness
- verified stock
- supplier cost vs final retail price
- shipping and delivery when verified
- taxes / duties when known
- market eligibility
- authenticity evidence
- return / cancellation clarity
- price-history signal when available
- comparable alternatives
- BOOM Red Team failure reasons
- final decision confidence

HUNT must never call something a great deal only because a discount percentage is shown.

## BOOM operating loop

MISSION → SCOUT → MATCH → VERIFY → LANDED COST → RED TEAM → COMPARE → DECIDE → WATCH → LEARN

## Competitive moat

### Outcome memory
After a real purchase, HUNT should learn from the real outcome:
final paid amount, delivery result, return outcome, and shopper feedback.

### Counterfactual shopping
BOOM can explain how the result changes if the shopper adjusts budget, color, delivery time, or another non-sensitive constraint.

### Trust-first ranking
Organic ranking is based on decision quality.
Sponsored placement must be labeled and kept separate from the BOOM verdict.

### Mission sharing
A useful mission result can become a shareable card:
what was requested, how many candidates were checked, why options were rejected, and the verified shortlist.

## BOOM governance

BOOM may research, classify, score, compare, Red Team, recommend, test, and learn from permitted shopping signals.

BOOM must not autonomously:
- spend advertising budget
- activate a paid supplier plan
- enable production checkout before fulfillment and payment controls are verified
- publish unsupported price, stock, discount, review, or popularity claims
- let sponsorship silently override the organic decision score

Owner approval remains required for paid commitments and production launches.

## Promo Truth and Deal Chess

HUNT promotions are not decorative marketing claims. They are structured, verifiable deal objects.

Supported deal families:
- percent or amount off
- 1+1 / BOGO
- Buy X Get Y
- bundle
- tiered / volume discount
- coupon
- free shipping
- free gift
- combined promotions

Before a campaign receives a VERIFIED DEAL label, HUNT requires a live status, valid time window, HTTPS source evidence, recent verification and checkout verification.

BOOM then evaluates mission fit:
- effective unit value when it can be calculated without assumptions
- quantity the shopper actually needs
- whether the promotion forces unnecessary overbuy
- final landed cost when shipping and taxes are available
- competing offers
- allowed promotion combinations

This layer is called **Deal Chess**:
BOOM searches the legal, supported promotion combinations and chooses the best outcome for the shopping mission. It must never assume discounts stack when the source checkout does not allow stacking.

### Channel note — 2026-09-09
