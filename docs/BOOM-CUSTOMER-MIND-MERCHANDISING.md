# BOOM Customer Mind & Merchandising — Operating Playbook

Purpose: understand shopping intent and present products in a way that reduces uncertainty and improves fit, without manipulation.

## 1. Read intent from behavior, not assumptions
Use only shopping signals such as:
- category viewed
- search terms
- price range
- device/model compatibility
- save/like/cart behavior
- preference for newness vs practical utility
- level of detail the shopper opens

Never infer sensitive traits or pretend to know a person's personality from weak signals.

## 2. Four practical shopper modes
- STYLE: wants novelty, visual identity and discovery.
- VALUE: wants a strong deal and clear price advantage.
- CONFIDENCE: wants proof, quality, reviews, returns and fit.
- SPEED: wants the best relevant option quickly.

BOOM may switch presentation when evidence from current-session behavior supports it.## 3. Presentation rules
For hedonic products such as jewelry, bags and fashion accessories:
- strong hero image
- lifestyle/context image
- detail close-up
- short video when available
- curated color/variant set
- fewer but stronger products above the fold

For utilitarian products such as phone cases, chargers and protectors:
- clean product-first image
- exact compatibility
- material/specs
- proof of function
- close-up of protection/ports/buttons
- shipping/returns and current availability

Avoid overcrowding. Separate alternatives visually and keep comparisons easy.

## 4. Trust layer
Show only verified claims.
Use real ratings/reviews when available.
Prefer review media tied to real buyers/platform evidence.
Explain why a product is shown when useful.
Never fabricate scarcity, countdowns, MSRP, stock, reviews or social proof.## 5. Offer logic
A promotion is shown only when supplier/marketplace terms support it.
Rank offers by:
1. relevance
2. quality gate
3. landed-value potential
4. availability
5. delivery fit
6. return risk
7. visual appeal

Never rank solely by commission.

## 6. Supplier-to-shopper translation
Supplier language becomes shopper language:
- material -> what it feels like / durability
- QC -> confidence
- magnet strength -> stable attachment
- plating/test docs -> wear/skin-contact confidence
- stitching/hardware -> finish quality
- lead time -> realistic delivery expectation

## 7. Research basis used for this playbook
Recent 2025–2026 e-commerce research supports adapting visual complexity to product type, reducing uncertainty with strong product presentation, and using clear visual separation to reduce choice deferral. BOOM should test these principles against HUNT's own analytics rather than assume universal effects.

BOOM rule: understand, clarify, present, verify — never pressure or manipulate.
