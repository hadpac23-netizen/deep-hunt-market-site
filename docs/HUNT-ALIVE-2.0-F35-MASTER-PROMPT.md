# HUNT ALIVE 2.0 — F35 MASTER PROMPT

## Role
Principal Brand Motion Designer + Commerce Experience Architect + Frontend Performance Engineer.

## Mission
Make HUNT feel unmistakably alive, premium, modern and joyful while preserving commerce truth, performance and accessibility.

## 1. Future app signature
Every public HUNT wordmark follows one global sequence:
1. H → U → N → T clearly drop from above with visible stagger.
2. A bright glass shimmer sweeps across the complete word after settlement.
3. A four-point star appears above the T, rotates through a full premium arc and settles above it.
4. The T performs a 3D spin and returns to the exact baseline.
5. After the T lands, a subtle smiling eye/wink and curved smile appear on the T.
6. The final expression is happy and memorable, not childish.
7. A rare micro-wink/twinkle is allowed after the sequence; the letters never continuously bounce.
8. Reduced-motion users receive the static final identity.

## 2. Night World
- Night-city visuals only.
- No sky-only, mountain or person-led hero scenes.
- Ask the CDN for 5120px derivatives when the source supports it.
- Runtime quality gate: reject anything below 3000×1800 or unsuitable landscape ratio.
- Mark >=4800px sources as ultra-5k and >=3000px as premium-3k.
- Use two image layers for cinematic crossfade.
- Each city gets its own restrained pan direction.
- Preload only the next scene during idle time.
- Never call a lower-resolution image “12K”; deliver a 12K-feel through clean high-resolution sources, contrast, depth and motion without false claims.

## 3. HUNT NOW
Maintain a living commerce discovery surface:
- For You Now
- Fresh Mix
- Complete the Look
- Switch the Vibe
- Verified Picks only when retail_price_verified=true AND Profit Gate PASS
- Ships to your market only when country evidence is verified

No fake trending, scarcity, discounts, prices or shipping.

## 4. HUNT Night Edit
A separate editorial lookbook surface built from real catalog data:
- Women
- Beauty
- Accessories
- Tech
- Home
- Men
- Three products at a time
- Large editorial lead card + two supporting cards
- Auto-change only while visible

- Price shown only when verified; otherwise show the existing live-price verification language

## 5. Motion hierarchy
1. HUNT brand signature = strongest motion
2. Night-city background = cinematic, slow
3. HUNT NOW / Night Edit = controlled mode transitions
4. Product cards = subtle hover/reveal only

No chaotic looping motion.

## 6. Safety / truth
- Payment remains PRELAUNCH until explicit owner approval.
- Supplier live ordering remains off until explicit owner approval.
- Preserve Account Memory, Country Brain, Product Pulse, Catalog Quality and i18n.

- T drop/spin/smile sequence visible.
- Star lands above T and rotates.
- 390/768/1280/1440: zero horizontal overflow.
- 7 languages and RTL preserved.
- Night city quality gate reports premium-3k or ultra-5k.
- HUNT NOW and Night Edit contain only real quality-gated products.
