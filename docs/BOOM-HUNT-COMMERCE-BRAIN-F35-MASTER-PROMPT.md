Use psychology to improve:
- cognitive fluency;
- relevance;
- curiosity;
- comprehension;
- trust;
- confidence;
- product comparison;
- visual delight;
- memory of the HUNT brand.

Never use:
- fear, shame or social pressure;
- fake urgency/scarcity/countdowns;
- addictive infinite reward loops;
- obscured costs;
- preselected consent;
- fake popularity or social proof;

- coercive checkout patterns.
