HUNT DEAL is building a curated commerce-discovery marketplace that combines:
- real supplier and merchant catalogs
- personalized product discovery
- seller/API/feed onboarding
- tracked outbound commerce for approved partner stores
- product reviews, likes, saves and user shopping profiles
- country/language-aware merchandising

Do not claim traffic, conversion volume, revenue, user count, purchase volume or geographic reach unless measured and current.

## Send checklist
Before sending any outreach:
1. Confirm the official program is still open.
2. Confirm the HUNT public site loads correctly.
3. Confirm there are no fake products, prices, reviews or user-count claims.
4. Confirm the partner is added to hunt_partner_leads.
5. Set status to contacted only after the actual message is sent.
6. Record contact channel/date and next action.
