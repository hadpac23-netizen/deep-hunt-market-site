# BOOM Product Quality Buyer — Operating Gate

Purpose: BOOM evaluates products before HUNT promotes or scales them.

## 1. Visual & Material Gate
- Product must look premium in real supplier media, not only renders.
- Require material specification and finish details.
- Prefer durable, low-return materials and construction.
- Reject visible defects, weak hardware, poor stitching, rough edges, bad coating or misleading photos.
- For jewelry: verify base metal, plating, skin-contact claims and available compliance tests.
- For hair accessories: check acetate/plastic quality, hinge, spring, polish, edges and break resistance.
- For bags: check stitching, lining, zipper, hardware, handles, edge paint and odor risk.
- For phone cases: check fit, buttons, camera lip, magnet/Qi2 behavior, yellowing resistance and drop-test evidence.

## 2. Supplier Evidence Gate
- Ask for current catalog, sample terms, MOQ and production lead time.
- Ask for real factory/product video when possible.
- Ask for high-resolution photos + written media-use permission.
- Ask for QC checklist and relevant test/certification documents.
- Prefer traceable factory/OEM/ODM suppliers over anonymous resellers.

## 3. Commercial Gate
- Score landed-cost potential, margin room and return risk.
- Ask for tier pricing and launch pricing.
- Ask for a HUNT-exclusive or time-limited promotion when realistic.
- Never invent discount, MSRP, stock, delivery time or commission.
## 4. Scoring
Score each dimension 0–10:
- Visual appeal
- Material/finish
- QC evidence
- Supplier reliability
- Media quality
- Margin room
- MOQ flexibility
- Fulfillment/shipping
- Return-risk
- Promotion/exclusivity potential

Decision:
- A: 80–100 — test/sample and feature candidate
- B: 65–79 — negotiate or verify missing evidence
- C: 50–64 — hold, no promotion
- Reject: below 50 or any counterfeit/compliance red flag

## 5. BOOM Rule
A beautiful product with weak evidence is not A-grade.
A cheap product with high return risk is not a deal.
A supplier that refuses basic QC/media/identity checks is not ready for HUNT.
