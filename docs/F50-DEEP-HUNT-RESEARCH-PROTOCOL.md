# F50 — DEEP HUNT RESEARCH PROTOCOL

## Mission
F50 is an extreme research and invention protocol whose output is exactly one unusually strong idea, or zero.

It is not an idea list and must not manufacture a winner merely to produce an answer.

## Required flow

Internal funnel:
50 → 10 → 3 → 1 or 0

## One-Idea Rule
External final output:
- one winner that passed every F50 hard gate, or
- zero.

Intermediate candidates are internal research material and are not presented as winners.

## Forbidden shortcuts
F50 must not:
- promote a medium-quality idea just to answer;
- call something globally novel without broad prior-art evidence;
- treat an ordinary AI agent, marketplace, CRM, chatbot, recommender, auction or payment layer as invention merely because it has a new wrapper;
- confuse product variation with mechanism novelty;
- treat synthetic assumptions as verified evidence;
- infer economics without a payer and economic primitive;
- claim scale as a promise.

## Evidence contract
A KEEP decision requires:
- multiple verified sources;
- at least two independent sources;
- prior-art coverage across products, startups, patents, research, GitHub, legacy industries and alternate names;
- explicit novelty claim bounded by evidence;
- Red Team;
- scale math;
- Big-Tech Copy Test;
- economic primitive and payer;
- technical and legal feasibility not known to fail.

## Execution boundary
F50 researches and recommends only.
It does not:
- spend money;
- publish externally;
- activate payment;
- place supplier orders;
- make external commitments.

Owner review remains required.

## Research Memory rule
Every sufficiently researched KILL/KEEP/WINNER receives a mechanism fingerprint built from normalized mechanism primitives.

Memory checks:
- exact fingerprint match;
- near-duplicate mechanism primitive overlap.

A previously killed mechanism is blocked by default.
Reopening requires:
- a substantive reopen reason; and
- at least two material new evidence references.

Known winners are not treated as new inventions; they require revalidation.

## Red-Team evidence rule
All required Red-Team dimensions must be attacked.
HIGH/FATAL risks cannot pass merely because a mitigation sentence exists; resolved high risks require substantive mitigation and an evidence reference.
