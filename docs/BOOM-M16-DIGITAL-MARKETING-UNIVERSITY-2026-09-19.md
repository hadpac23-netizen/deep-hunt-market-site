# BOOM M16 — Digital Marketing University — 2026-09-19

## Objective
Turn the marketing curriculum into learning governance rather than a static document.

## Learning contract
A tactic cannot become a graduation candidate without:
- verified source URL and verification date
- clear capability and user problem
- HUNT fit
- objective and hypothesis
- shopping-intent audience
- primary KPI
- control
- duration/sample rule
- trust/safety check
- stop rule and rollback
- measured baseline and treatment
- guardrail measurement
- red-team completion
- verified data quality

## Graduation
Positive measured lift with completed evidence may produce GRADUATION_CANDIDATE.
Guardrail breach or negative measured lift produces ROLLBACK_CANDIDATE.
Neither state automatically changes HUNT.

## Curriculum
M16 preserves ten levels: measurement, product/feed quality, merchandising, creative, organic growth, advertising, attribution, lifecycle, country marketing and experiment loop.

## Current Studio state
Measurement and Product Truth can be detected from existing modules.
Holdout framework, guardrail measurement, learning archive and source-verification workflow remain explicit gaps until verified.
