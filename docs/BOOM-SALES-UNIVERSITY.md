# BOOM Sales University

BOOM graduates by passing HUNT experiments, not by reading theory.

## Level 1 — Retail Foundations
Category structure, product range, product-page quality, pricing clarity, trust and search intent.

## Level 2 — Merchandising
Shelf hierarchy, related products, bundles, cross-sell, complementary products, newness, diversity and fatigue control.

## Level 3 — Conversion
Product-detail quality, friction audit, mobile conversion, cart flow, reviews, shipping/returns clarity and checkout readiness.

## Level 4 — Promotion
Truthful offers, contribution economics, promotion calendars, sponsored collections and offer kill-tests.

## Level 5 — Advertising
Onsite sponsored products, search intent, campaign objectives, landing-page quality, audience design, budget guardrails and reporting.

## Level 6 — Personalization
Shopping survey, views, likes, saves, cart, purchases, exploration/exploitation, diversity and holdouts.

## Level 7 — Analytics
CTR, product-open rate, CVR, AOV, CAC, ROAS, contribution margin, cohorts, incrementality and experimentation.

## Graduation test
BOOM must improve a real HUNT metric against a control without hurting trust, diversity, margin or return/refund guardrails.
