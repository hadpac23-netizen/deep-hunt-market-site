# HUNT 2037 — Master Blueprint

Status: Product North Star / Source of Truth
Date: 2026-09-18
Owner: HUNT / BOOM
Principle: HUNT must never feel like a conventional ecommerce grid.

## 1. Origin of the idea

HUNT 2037 evolved from a simple Brand Factory experiment:
- Start with verified supplier products.
- Turn selected product images into short, modern motion/video creatives.
- Preserve product truth: no invented claims, materials, compatibility, prices, stock, or delivery promises.
- Use BOOM Studio to test creative hooks before spending heavily on generated media.

The idea expanded when we reviewed premium phone cases and HyperSKU:
- The supplier catalog itself contained many visually strong products.
- Instead of importing a static catalog, HUNT should continuously discover, select, present, learn, and change.
- The storefront becomes a living shopping world rather than a product database.

## 2. North Star

HUNT is not a catalog.
HUNT is not a clone of TikTok, SHEIN, Temu, Amazon, or Pinterest.
HUNT is a dynamic discovery world that rebuilds itself around each shopper.
## 3. Experience principles

1. Opening HUNT should feel like entering a world, not opening a store.
2. Every session may look different.
3. Two users should rarely receive the same sequence of products.
4. Product truth and checkout clarity stay simple even when discovery is expressive.
5. Search remains fast and obvious for users who already know what they want.
6. Discovery is controlled surprise, not meaningless randomness.
7. Motion, video, still imagery, carousels, moodboards and interactive cards are mixed intentionally.
8. The interface should feel light and learnable like iOS: low friction, clear gestures, minimal cognitive load.
9. The system may change layouts, rhythm, scale, background tone and visual world as context changes.
10. No fake urgency, fake scarcity, fake discounts or fabricated social proof.

## 4. BOOM Discovery Engine

BOOM chooses the next experience unit from:
- personalized products,
- adjacent tastes,
- NEW arrivals,
- trending items,
- editorial picks,
- sponsored discoveries that pass quality gates,
- controlled wildcards for exploration.

The feed should not be a fixed 4-column grid.
Possible units:
- full-screen short video,
- cinematic hero product,
- 2–4 product cluster,
- outfit / complete-the-look composition,
- jewelry close-up,
- shoppable moodboard,
- NEW DROP interstitial,
- product comparison,
- interactive product motion,
- category/world portal.
## 5. HUNT Worlds

Categories become living worlds rather than static navigation pages:
- Fashion World
- Jewelry World
- Beauty World
- Tech World
- iPhone World
- Home World
- Sports World
- Kids World
- Pet World
- Surprise World

Transitions between worlds can change:
- background tone,
- typography scale,
- motion language,
- card geometry,
- ambient visual treatment,
- product density,
- content format.

A click does not always open a new page.
Sometimes the current world expands around the user's action.

Example:
Jewelry like → more earrings → rings → necklace → outfit context → "Style around this".
iPhone case like → cases → charging → desk setup → accessories → tech surprise.

## 6. Country Brain / Shipping Chess

Country context is evaluated before ranking products.
For every user/session HUNT considers:
- country,
- supplier warehouse location,
- SKU stock by warehouse,
- shipping method,
- landed cost,
- delivery estimate,
- restrictions,
- margin,
- return feasibility.

Products from nearby or operationally better warehouses should receive priority when quality and relevance are comparable.
Never claim "local stock" unless the exact SKU is verified in that warehouse.
## 7. Supplier Brain

Primary direction:
- HyperSKU as a major quality/sourcing/fulfillment source.
- CJdropshipping as a major additional source.
- EPROLO as an additional source.
- Future official suppliers may be added behind the same interface.

Supplier rules:
- official API/feed/integration only,
- no scraping as a core inventory method,
- per-SKU price/stock/shipping freshness,
- variants and compatibility must be explicit,
- quality gate before broad exposure,
- country-aware supplier selection.

BOOM may compare the same or equivalent product across suppliers and choose the best operational route based on:
quality + landed cost + stock + delivery + margin + reliability.

HyperSKU must not automatically replace every other supplier.
HUNT should orchestrate suppliers rather than depend blindly on one.

## 8. Product Truth Gate

Before a product can become LIVE:
- exact product identity,
- exact variant,
- current supplier cost,
- current inventory,
- destination shipping quote,
- delivery estimate,
- verified images,
- material/compatibility claims,
- margin threshold,
- country eligibility.

If freshness expires, status becomes RECHECK REQUIRED rather than silently remaining "verified".
## 9. Taste DNA

BOOM learns preferences from behavior such as:
- views,
- dwell time,
- likes,
- saves,
- shares,
- history revisits,
- category/world entry,
- add-to-cart,
- purchase,
- skips,
- explicit dislike / not interested.

Taste DNA is multi-dimensional, not just Men/Women:
minimal, street, elegant, colorful, luxury, tech, classic, playful, etc.

Users should be able to influence or reset their preferences.
Personalization must help discovery, not trap users in a filter bubble.

## 10. HUNT Memory

Every user receives visual viewing history:
- Today
- Yesterday
- This Week
- Saved
- Liked
- Shared
- Purchased

BOOM can surface useful reminders:
- "You viewed this yesterday."
- "Your size/color is back."
- "This item changed price."
- "Want to continue where you left off?"

Natural-language recall should be supported:
"Show me the black jeans I saw two days ago."
## 11. Share Graph + Referral Loop

Every product, look, world and HUNT itself gets a Share action.

Share objects should be attractive HUNT cards, not plain URLs.
Future social actions:
- send to friend,
- ask "Which one?",
- vote,
- Shop Together,
- shared wishlist.

Referral rewards:
- a verified referral can earn points,
- rewards must be transparent,
- no fake contests or manipulative countdowns,
- anti-abuse rules are required,
- points can unlock legitimate benefits defined by HUNT economics.

Sharing becomes part of discovery:
a friend's reactions can influence suggestions only with appropriate consent.

## 12. HUNT Watch

One tap can ask HUNT to watch:
- price,
- stock,
- size,
- color,
- shipping,
- supplier route.

Notifications should be useful and sparse:
"Your size is back" is valuable.
Generic spam is not.
## 13. BOOM Stylist

BOOM becomes a style director and shopping assistant.

Core actions:
- Style this for me
- Complete the Look
- Dress me for...
- Build 3 styles: Casual / Elegant / Street
- Build a look under a budget
- Build around one saved item
- Suggest missing pieces from items the user already owns or selects

The stylist should focus on preferences, context and product coordination.
It should not judge bodies or pressure users toward appearance ideals.

Cross-sell should feel like styling assistance, not forced upselling.

## 14. HUNT Agent

The user can give missions:
- "Build me an outfit under $120."
- "Find three gifts."
- "Build a home-office setup under $300."
- "Find a premium iPhone case with fast shipping."

The agent evaluates:
product truth + destination + budget + taste + inventory + shipping + margin.

The result may dynamically rebuild the storefront around the mission.
## 15. Creative Engine / Brand Factory

BOOM Studio turns selected real products into richer discovery units:
- short video,
- image-to-motion,
- zoom/reveal,
- product orbit,
- moodboard,
- outfit composition,
- editorial card,
- NEW DROP visual.

Start with approximately 50 Hero Products, not thousands of generated videos.

For each hero product:
- keep supplier reference image,
- generate/test 2–3 presentation styles,
- run visual fidelity QA,
- preserve exact product truth,
- publish only approved creatives.

Generated visuals must not invent:
materials, compatibility, logos, certifications, performance claims, stock, price or shipping promises.

## 16. NEW Engine

NEW is not only a badge.
A newly verified product can trigger:
- NEW card,
- NEW DROP sequence,
- category/world expansion,
- personalized "New in your world" collection.

A product loses NEW status according to defined freshness rules.
## 17. Dynamic Layout Engine

The storefront can change presentation format while preserving usability.

Mobile:
- vertical discovery flow,
- full-screen or near-full-screen moments,
- swipe/scroll,
- clear Like / Save / Share / Shop actions.

Desktop:
- Living Canvas rather than oversized mobile TikTok clone,
- large hero areas,
- asymmetric editorial groups,
- visible hints of what comes next,
- smooth transitions between worlds.

The engine may vary:
- item size,
- layout density,
- background,
- motion,
- content type,
- category sequence.

But checkout, product facts, size/variant controls and accessibility remain stable.

## 18. Discovery mix

Initial experimentation can use a controlled mix such as:
- majority personalized,
- some adjacent-interest content,
- some NEW/trending,
- some controlled surprise.

Percentages are experiments, not permanent rules.
BOOM optimizes against useful signals such as:
engagement, saves, return visits, carts, purchases, refunds and long-term satisfaction.
## 19. Monetization

Potential revenue layers:
- retail margin,
- intelligent bundles,
- Complete-the-Look baskets,
- supplier routing optimization,
- merchant promotion,
- clearly labeled sponsored discovery,
- future premium merchant services.

Sponsored placement never bypasses Product Truth / Quality Gates.

Profit formula must use real destination economics:
sale price
- supplier cost
- shipping
- payment fees
- taxes/duties where applicable
- returns/risk reserve
= contribution margin.

No guaranteed profit claims.

## 20. BOOM Studio control plane

BOOM Studio should expose:
- supplier connections,
- country/warehouse status,
- product truth status,
- quality score,
- margin score,
- creative status,
- NEW status,
- personalization eligibility,
- sponsored status,
- world/category mapping,
- experiment assignment,
- owner approval gates.

Owner control remains explicit for:
production activation, paid generation, publishing, live payments and sensitive supplier actions.

## 22. Success definition

A user should open HUNT and immediately feel:
"This is not another store."

They should want to:
- keep discovering,
- save things,
- come back to history,
- share products/worlds,
- ask BOOM for help,
- complete looks/missions,
- return because HUNT remembers useful context.

Final product statement:
**HUNT 2037 = Memory + Taste + Worlds + Discovery + Stylist + Agent + Country Brain + Commerce.**
