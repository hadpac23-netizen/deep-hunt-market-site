# BOOM Unknown Knowledge Map

Purpose: track what HUNT still does not know well enough, learn it deliberately, and convert learning into operating gates.

## Tier 1 — Must know before scaling
1. True landed economics per SKU
   - supplier price
   - payment fees
   - shipping
   - duties/VAT/tax handling
   - return/refund cost
   - expected defect/chargeback cost
   - marketing cost
   - net contribution margin

2. Supplier reliability under scale
   - on-time rate
   - defect rate
   - stock accuracy
   - response speed
   - replacement/refund behavior
   - production capacity
   - feed freshness

3. Product quality evidence
   - sample inspection
   - materials
   - construction/finish
   - safety/compliance documents
   - real photos/video
   - repeat-batch consistency
## Tier 2 — Marketplace operating knowledge
4. Global product compliance
   - EU GPSR / Safety Gate obligations
   - seller/trader traceability
   - category-specific chemical/material restrictions
   - recalls and takedown workflow
   - country-by-country labeling/warnings

5. Commerce tax/customs
   - EU OSS/IOSS
   - import VAT/duties
   - destination-specific thresholds and collection logic
   - who is importer/seller of record

6. Returns, disputes and fraud
   - return windows
   - reverse logistics
   - damaged/not-as-described workflows
   - chargeback evidence
   - supplier liability allocation

7. Authenticity/IP
   - trademark authorization
   - brand distribution rights
   - counterfeit screening
   - media/license rights
   - takedown/escalation process
## Tier 3 — Growth intelligence
8. Customer intent
   - STYLE / VALUE / CONFIDENCE / SPEED modes
   - search-to-click and click-to-cart patterns
   - price sensitivity
   - variant/size/compatibility friction
   - reason for abandonment

9. Merchandising
   - best hero image type by category
   - ideal number of choices
   - video impact
   - bundle performance
   - trust information placement
   - category-specific ranking rules

10. Promotion science
   - true supplier-funded discount
   - bundle economics
   - limited offers only when real
   - margin floor
   - incremental conversion vs cannibalization

11. Supplier negotiation
   - human tone
   - proof-based persuasion
   - sample/test order
   - tier pricing
   - launch offer
   - exclusivity only after proof
## Tier 4 — Technical product knowledge
12. Phone accessories
   - exact model compatibility
   - case fit/button/camera tolerances
   - anti-yellowing evidence
   - drop protection claims
   - Qi/Qi2 certification when charging is involved
   - Apple accessory/MFi requirements where applicable

13. Jewelry/accessories
   - nickel release and skin-contact risks
   - lead/cadmium restrictions where applicable
   - plating/PVD durability
   - clasp/hinge/spring durability
   - coating consistency

14. BOOM learning loop
For every unknown:
- FIND authoritative sources
- VERIFY date/jurisdiction
- TEST on samples/data
- RECORD evidence
- SCORE confidence
- UPDATE gate/ranking
- RED TEAM failure modes
- RECHECK when source/market changes

Rule: BOOM must distinguish KNOWN, INFERRED, UNVERIFIED, and BLOCKED.
No SKU, supplier, claim or promotion is upgraded on inference alone.
