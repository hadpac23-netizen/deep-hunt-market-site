# BOOM Country Launch Matrix

## Wave 1 — highest practical focus
### United States
Why: large ecommerce demand, strong affiliate/creator ecosystem, many supplier programs.
Needs: USD pricing, shipping/returns truth, US SEO/content, tax/merchant readiness where applicable.

### United Kingdom
Why: strong fashion/beauty/luxury demand and English content reuse.
Needs: GBP, UK shipping/returns and VAT/merchant truth.

### Germany
Why: strong ecommerce market and European supplier proximity.
Needs: German localization, EUR pricing, EU returns/consumer compliance.

### Canada
Why: English/French opportunity and strong North American ecommerce behavior.
Needs: CAD, shipping/returns clarity, later French localization.

### Australia
Why: strong ecommerce penetration and good fit for fashion/home/POD.
Needs: AUD, shipping-time accuracy and returns.

## Wave 2
France, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Sweden, Denmark, Singapore, Japan, South Korea, UAE.

## Supplier-source regions
Europe: luxury/fashion, POD, home, beauty.
US/Canada: beauty/fragrance, affiliate, retail partners, creator commerce.
Australia: local fashion/lifestyle and POD.
China: broad safe-product supply only through approved feeds/APIs; strict authenticity/safety filtering.
East/Southeast Asia: fashion/accessories/beauty/home through verified programs and feeds.

## Launch scoring
Each country receives 1–5 for:
- supplier coverage
- shipping speed/cost
- returns complexity
- payment readiness
- tax/compliance readiness
- organic search opportunity
- localization effort
- product-market fit
- partner availability
- expected contribution margin

BOOM should not call a country “launched” until shipping/returns/payment/merchant truth is complete.
