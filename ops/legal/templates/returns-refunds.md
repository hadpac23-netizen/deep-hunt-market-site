Status: DRAFT — NOT CUSTOMER-FACING.

## Return Authorization
Customers start a return by contacting HUNT Support with the order reference, item, reason, and evidence where relevant. HUNT issues a Return Authorization reference and verified return or collection instructions for that order. Customers should not self-ship to a supplier or other address unless HUNT confirms the route or mandatory law provides otherwise.

## Mandatory consumer rights
This policy does not reduce rights that cannot legally be waived. For Israeli distance-sale consumers, the final published policy must preserve applicable cancellation rights and statutory exceptions. Where cancellation results from defect, non-conformity, or failure to supply on time, HUNT will follow the legally required no-fee / collection or equivalent handling rather than applying the ordinary return route.

## Refund handling
Once the applicable cancellation/return requirements are met, HUNT records the evidence and processes the refund or replacement according to the published policy and mandatory law. Refund timing, cancellation fees, return-shipping responsibility, and exceptions must follow the applicable market rules.
