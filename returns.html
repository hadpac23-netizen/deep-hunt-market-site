RETURNS · CANCELLATION · WITHDRAWAL

Mandatory rights come first.

HUNT will not use a store policy to take away cancellation, withdrawal, warranty or refund rights that local law gives the customer.

EU/EEA distance sales

Where EU consumer law applies, consumers generally have a 14-day statutory withdrawal period for distance purchases of goods, beginning after delivery, subject to legal exceptions. The customer must be informed in advance about return costs and other required information. Mandatory remedies for faulty or misdescribed goods are separate from change-of-mind withdrawal rights.

Israel distance transactions

Where Israeli consumer law applies, distance-selling cancellation rights and statutory exceptions/fees apply according to the Consumer Protection Law and regulations. HUNT will apply the mandatory rule in force for the transaction rather than a less favorable store rule.

Other countries

If local mandatory law gives you stronger cancellation, return, warranty or refund rights, those rights prevail. Checkout should present destination-specific information before payment when the real-money flow is activated.

Return condition and exceptions

Change-of-mind returns may be subject to lawful conditions and statutory exceptions, including certain personalized, perishable, hygiene-sealed or other legally excluded goods. Faulty, unsafe, counterfeit, misdescribed or incorrectly supplied goods are handled under applicable mandatory rights and supplier/merchant responsibility.

Return costs and address

Who pays return shipping depends on the reason for return and mandatory law. HUNT must disclose any customer-paid return cost before purchase where required. Do not send goods to an unconfirmed address.

Refund timing

Refunds will be processed within the mandatory timeframe applicable to the transaction once the legal conditions for refund are met. Where law allows, reimbursement may be held until returned goods or evidence of return is received.
