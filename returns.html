1. Start with Return Authorization

To request a cancellation, return, replacement or refund, contact hacoachle@gmail.com with the order reference, item and reason. Where relevant, include evidence of a damaged, defective or wrong item. HUNT will verify the order and provide a Return Authorization reference plus the correct return or collection instruction for that order.

Do not self-ship an item to a supplier or another address unless HUNT has confirmed that route, except where mandatory law gives you a different right.

2. Israeli distance-sale baseline

For Israeli consumers in a distance-sale transaction, applicable law generally provides a 14-day cancellation right subject to statutory rules and exceptions. For an ordinary eligible cancellation, the cancellation fee is generally limited to 5% of the transaction price or NIS 100, whichever is lower. Where cancellation is due to a defect, non-conformity or failure to supply on time, HUNT will not charge that ordinary cancellation fee where the law prohibits it and will follow the collection/return handling required by law.

3. Other markets

Customers outside Israel receive the cancellation and return rights required by the law applicable to their transaction. If local mandatory law gives stronger rights than this policy, the stronger rights apply.

4. Refund timing and method

When a cancellation/refund is legally due and the required return or collection conditions are satisfied, HUNT will process the refund within the period required by applicable law. For Israeli distance-sale cancellations, the statutory baseline generally requires refunding within 14 days of receiving the cancellation notice, subject to the applicable legal conditions and exceptions. Refunds are normally returned through the original payment method unless another method is required or agreed lawfully.

5. Damaged, defective, wrong or missing items

Contact Support promptly. HUNT will verify the order and available evidence, then provide the appropriate replacement, collection, refund or other remedy required by the transaction and applicable law. Do not discard an item that may need inspection unless Support confirms otherwise.

6. Cross-border returns

The correct return destination may depend on the item, warehouse, destination and legal requirements. HUNT therefore uses order-specific return instructions instead of publishing a universal warehouse address. This operational model does not reduce mandatory consumer rights.

7. Pre-launch notice

No real-money purchases are currently accepted on the Pre-Launch checkout, so no new paid order can presently enter this returns flow.

These policies are intended to operate alongside mandatory consumer and privacy law. Where applicable law grants stronger rights, those rights prevail. Before real-money checkout is enabled, HUNT will publish the additional transaction disclosures required for the active market, including the registered business address where required.
