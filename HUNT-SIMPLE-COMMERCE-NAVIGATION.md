# HUNT SIMPLE COMMERCE NAVIGATION

Goal: keep HUNT cinematic while making shopping navigation immediate.

Rules:
- Keep Main Categories visible in one horizontal row.
- Selecting a Main Category shows its Departments directly underneath.
- Departments are text-only, compact, and remain in one horizontal row.
- On narrow screens, both rows may scroll horizontally.
- Keep the selected Main Category and Department visibly active.
- Do not use large cards, thumbnails, counts, full-screen navigation, modal navigation, or tall multi-row menus by default.
- Keep the Living Campaign directly below navigation.
- Products remain below the campaign and browse vertically.
- Keep Sort and Filter in the product-list area, separate from Category and Department navigation.
- Search complements browsing; it does not replace Categories.
- Preserve the exact HUNT route Main Category -> Department -> Product Shelf.
- HUNT branding and cinematic visual language remain original.

Decision rule:
If a navigation interaction needs explanation, simplify it.
