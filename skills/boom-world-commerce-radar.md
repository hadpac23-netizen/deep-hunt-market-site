# BOOM World Commerce Radar

## Mission
Continuously scan official retail, marketplace, search, social-commerce, advertising, creator-commerce and AI-shopping sources for capabilities that can materially improve HUNT.

## Required output for every finding
- capability, not hype
- official source + verification date
- user problem solved
- HUNT fit
- implementation complexity 1-5
- user value 1-5
- privacy/safety/trust risks
- dependencies
- prototype plan
- kill test
- measurable success metric

## Priority domains
1. product discovery and conversational shopping
2. visual search and shop-the-look
3. shoppable video / LIVE / creator commerce
4. price watch and verified deal intelligence
5. merchant feeds and catalog normalization
6. personalization with explicit shopper controls
7. product review trust and summaries
8. advertising automation with owner approval
9. localization, language, currency and country merchandising
10. fulfillment, shipping, returns and post-purchase tracking

## Sources to monitor
Prefer official product/news/developer sources from Google Shopping/Search, Amazon retail/Ads, TikTok Shop, Shopify, Pinterest, Meta, Snap, YouTube Shopping, major retailers and verified commerce platforms. Community findings are leads only until verified.

## Guardrails
- no fake discounts, urgency, popularity, reviews or stock
- no sensitive-trait targeting
- no autonomous paid ad spend without owner approval
- no autonomous paid checkout until HUNT payment/tax/shipping/returns/legal controls are verified
- no unlicensed fashion media ingestion
- no brand inventory labeled official/connected until source/partnership is verified
- visual/try-on features require explicit privacy controls and may not rate bodies or reinforce appearance ideals

## Loop
SCAN -> VERIFY -> SCORE -> RED TEAM -> PROTOTYPE -> A/B TEST -> ADOPT/ROLLBACK -> DOCUMENT.
