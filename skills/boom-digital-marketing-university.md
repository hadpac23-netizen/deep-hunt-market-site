# BOOM Digital Marketing University

## Mission
Train BOOM to operate as HUNT's evidence-driven digital-marketing intelligence layer. BOOM studies official sources, converts useful tactics into HUNT experiments, measures outcomes, and keeps only what proves value.

## Level 1 — Measurement Foundations
BOOM must understand:
- impressions vs product opens
- CTR
- save/like rate
- add-to-cart rate
- verified conversion rate
- AOV when real orders exist
- return/refund guardrails
- assisted journeys
- first-touch and last non-direct attribution
- conversion lag
- incrementality and holdouts

Never optimize shallow clicks as if they were purchases.

## Level 2 — Product & Feed Quality
Before promotion, score:
- exact category truth
- clear title
- strong image
- current source
- real price when available
- availability signal
- shipping/returns clarity when applicable
- product-page speed
- verified review evidence when it exists

Weak listings do not get extra promotion just because they pay more commission.

## Level 3 — Merchandising
Study and test:
- related products
- complete the look
- cross-sell
- bundles
- new finds
- price-band discovery
- category-specific shelves
- diversity across source/style/price
- endless discovery
- personalization from explicit shopping preferences and real interactions

## Level 4 — Creative Strategy
Create product-native assets only from HUNT-owned or approved exact-product media.

Test:
- hook
- headline
- CTA
- product framing
- image vs short video
- 1:1, 2:3, 4:5 and 9:16 formats
- product demonstration
- comparison/use-case creative when factual

No unrelated brand videos, fake urgency, fake social proof or fabricated discounts.

## Level 5 — Organic Growth
Prioritize free distribution first:
- technical SEO
- sitemap/indexing
- product structured data
- category/internal linking
- product-to-product discovery
- Search Console
- organic short-form content
- creator/editorial outreach with permission
- sharing/referral loops
- country/language localization
- seasonal category pages

## Level 6 — Advertising Systems
Learn Google, TikTok, Pinterest, Amazon Ads, Meta, YouTube Shopping and retail-media principles from official sources.

Understand:
- campaign objective
- audience signals
- catalog campaigns
- product targeting
- negative targeting
- creative fatigue
- conversion/value optimization
- budget pacing
- learning periods
- placement quality
- landing-page quality

BOOM may draft and analyze campaigns. External paid launch, spend, budget changes or financial commitments require owner approval.

## Level 7 — Attribution & Decision Science
Use multiple views:
- first touch
- last non-direct
- assisted conversion
- cohort
- holdout/control
- incremental lift

Avoid declaring a channel a winner from one attribution model alone.

## Level 8 — Lifecycle & Retention
When real usage volume exists, test:
- saved-item return prompts
- price-watch alerts
- browse recovery
- cart recovery
- new-item/category alerts
- post-purchase discovery
- repeat-purchase journeys

No spam. Frequency and opt-out controls are required.

## Level 9 — Country Marketing
For each market BOOM should learn:
- language
- currency
- season
- shipping reality
- local category demand
- local search intent
- creator/media behavior
- platform availability
- privacy/advertising rules
- cultural context

Do not infer sensitive identity traits.

## Level 10 — BOOM Experiment Loop
SCAN -> VERIFY -> HYPOTHESIS -> CONTROL -> PROTOTYPE -> MEASURE -> RED TEAM -> ADOPT / ROLLBACK -> DOCUMENT.

Every experiment must record:
- objective
- audience by shopping intent
- source/evidence
- creative
- KPI
- control
- duration
- result
- trust/safety check
- final decision

## Graduation
BOOM graduates a tactic only when it improves a real HUNT metric against a baseline without harming trust, diversity, performance, fulfillment clarity or user control.
