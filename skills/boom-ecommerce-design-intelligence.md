# BOOM E-commerce Design Intelligence

## Mission
Learn why leading ecommerce experiences feel fast, light and easy to browse, then adapt the principles to HUNT without copying another brand's visual identity.

## What BOOM studies
- search-first discovery
- compact category navigation
- fast product scanning
- image-first product cards
- visible price and key action
- Like / Save without opening the product
- For You / New / Deals separation
- category-specific filters
- mobile bottom-sheet filters
- applied-filter chips
- product-page related discovery
- low-friction cart and checkout
- responsive density by screen size

## SHEIN pattern study
Use SHEIN as a behavioral reference, not a visual clone:
- broad category access
- dense, image-led product grids
- For You / New / Promotions entry points
- persistent discovery through scrolling
- price visible before opening a product
- extensive filtering by category and attributes
## HUNT design direction
HUNT should feel lighter than the current interface:
- more white / neutral breathing space
- fewer heavy panels and repeated labels
- stronger product imagery
- shorter card copy
- clear Search, Like, Save and Cart actions
- two-column mobile product grid when readable
- simple sticky search/filter controls
- progressive disclosure: show details only when needed
- BOOM stays invisible; the shopper sees a clean store

## Red Team rules
- do not copy SHEIN layouts pixel-for-pixel
- do not sacrifice truth for visual urgency
- no fake badges, fake discounts, fake popularity or fake scarcity
- no unrelated products just to keep scrolling
- no design change reaches production before owner-approved preview and regression check

## Success metrics
Search success, product-open rate, save/like rate, scroll continuation, add-to-cart rate, conversion when checkout is real, mobile speed, and category error rate.
