## F60T-36 — Profit Preservation

For every proposed action compare:

EXPECTED NET PROFIT — ACTION

against:

EXPECTED NET PROFIT — BASELINE

Calculate:

EXPECTED INCREMENTAL NET PROFIT

Check:

cannibalization
coupon leakage
affiliate leakage
discount leakage
paid traffic replacing organic traffic
unnecessary free shipping
creator commission leakage
margin loss

If incremental net profit <= 0:

HOLD / KILL.

Protect existing profitable winners.

## F60T-37 — Price Pressure

Crowd strength is only one input to pricing.

Never raise price merely because traffic increased.

Require:

elasticity evidence
conversion evidence
margin evidence
product truth
shipping/stock truth

Output:

KEEP
TEST_RAISE
RAISE_CANDIDATE
ROLLBACK
HOLD

Live price changes remain Owner Gate unless explicitly pre-approved.
