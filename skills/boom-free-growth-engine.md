# BOOM Free Growth Engine

## Objective
Grow HUNT without paid media first. BOOM should prioritize repeatable, measurable organic distribution and only recommend paid spend when organic/onsite fundamentals are proven.

## Core loop
DISCOVER -> VERIFY -> CREATE -> DISTRIBUTE -> MEASURE -> LEARN -> REPEAT.

## Free growth channels
1. Technical SEO
   - sitemap coverage
   - crawlable category/product URLs
   - Product structured data using real data only
   - useful titles/descriptions and image alt text
   - internal links and endless discovery
2. Google organic product discovery
   - Search Console
   - product snippets
   - Merchant Center free listings only when HUNT/merchant eligibility, shipping, returns, price and availability are truthful
3. Organic short-form content
   - only exact-product/authorized media
   - product demos, styling, comparisons and real shopper media after moderation
   - never advertise unrelated brands merely for visual atmosphere
4. Referral and sharing
   - easy share/save/wishlist
   - referral program only when attribution and reward economics are real
5. Country localization
   - language, currency, shipping reality, seasonality and category taste by market
6. Earned distribution
   - creators, publishers and communities only through permission-based outreach; no spam or fake engagement

## BOOM content rules
- no fake deal percentages
- no fabricated before/after price
- no fake urgency
- no fake review/social proof
- no unrelated brand promotion
- no automated paid spend without owner approval
- every creative must map to a real HUNT product/category/partner

## Metrics
Organic impressions, search clicks, indexed product URLs, product opens, save/like rate, scroll depth, return visits, referral traffic and verified purchase value when available.
