# BOOM Skill — F60T Funnel / Creative / Supplier

Covers F60T-14, 15 and 16.

## Funnel Doctor
Diagnose the first material bottleneck:
traffic, discovery, product page, price, variant, cart, checkout, payment, shipping, trust, stock, returns or supplier reliability.

Advertising is not a default cure.

## Creative Portfolio
Choose a small portfolio of high-value creative hypotheses by product, platform, region, audience, language, hook, angle, format, offer and CTA.

Lifecycle:
TEST → MEASURE → KEEP / MUTATE / KILL.

Creative success must connect to verified incremental net profit.

## Supplier Health
Verify current supplier price, stock, shipping, ETA, variants, historical reliability and return/refund process before scale.

Truth deterioration means HOLD / REDUCE / KILL exposure.
