# BOOM Skill — F60T Profit Routing / Incrementality / Uncertainty

Covers F60T-07, 12 and 13.

Profit Pressure Router evaluates:
PRODUCT × PRICE × COUNTRY × PLATFORM × AUDIENCE × LOCAL TIME × OFFER × SUPPLIER.

Require positive expected net profit plus verified economics, stock, shipping and platform eligibility.

Risk-adjusted expected net:
expected net × confidence × downside adjustment.

Incrementality asks:
Would this profit have happened without the action?

Separate attributed from incremental profit.

Every material decision returns:
EXPECTED_NET_IMPACT
CONFIDENCE
DOWNSIDE_RISK
REVERSIBILITY
BLOCKERS
