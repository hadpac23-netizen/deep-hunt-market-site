# BOOM Creator OS Skill

## Mission
Control creator-commerce eligibility by rights, Product Truth, economics and confirmed attribution before HUNT publishes creator content, buys creator traffic or pays creator commission.

## Core principle
Creator popularity is not evidence of profitability.
Commission size is not a ranking signal.
A creator becomes a commercial candidate only when the exact content, market, channel, economics and attribution path are verified.

## Rights ledger
Every creator asset must eventually record:
- creator_id
- platform
- content asset identity
- usage_rights_verified
- allowed channels
- allowed countries / GLOBAL
- rights expiry
- paid-media permission
- edit/remix permission

Hold when:
- rights are unverified
- asset identity is unverified
- rights expired
- requested channel is not licensed
- requested country is not licensed
- paid-media rights are missing
- remix/edit rights are missing when modification is required

Partner media rights do not automatically equal creator rights.

## Product Truth
Creator content must use the same Commerce Passport and AI Claim Firewall as other HUNT creatives.
Unsupported product, delivery, compatibility, discount, popularity or performance claims are blocked.

## Creator economics
Creator economics must include, when applicable:
- product contribution before creator costs
- creator commission
- affiliate commission
- coupon cost
- platform fee
- content fee
- refund reserve
- chargeback reserve
- support/service cost

Missing costs are UNKNOWN, never zero.

Net contribution after creator costs must stay positive before a creator can become a test candidate.

## Attribution
Creator performance requires a creator attribution key and:
- persistent event identity
- click/referral identity
- confirmed conversion source
- confirmed conversions only

Never call a sale, revenue amount or commission “creator-generated” from clicks, views, saves, draft UTMs or generic merchant conversions alone.

## States
- DRAFT
- RIGHTS_HOLD
- ECONOMICS_HOLD
- ATTRIBUTION_HOLD
- OWNER_HOLD
- TEST_CANDIDATE
- SCALE_CANDIDATE

## Scale evidence
Scaling requires:
- minimum confirmed conversion sample
- observed net contribution after creator costs
- observed refund rate
- observed chargeback rate
- positive observed creator contribution

## Privacy / fairness
Do not rank or target creators using sensitive personal traits.
Use only commercial evidence relevant to the campaign: rights, channel, market, content evidence, product fit, measured conversions and economics.

## Execution
Creator OS is a decision layer.
It always keeps:
- publish=false
- payout=false
- execute=false

Creator outreach, publishing, boosting, contracts and payouts require separate approved workflows and owner review.
