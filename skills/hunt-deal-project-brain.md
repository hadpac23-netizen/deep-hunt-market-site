# HUNT Project Brain

## Identity
HUNT is BOOM's custom commerce/discovery marketplace. The user experience should feel like one intelligent HUNT system rather than a patchwork of supplier sites.

## Durable Commerce Rules
- ONSITE_FIRST checkout whenever legitimately possible.
- Zero-inventory/no-upfront-stock launch model.
- No fake products, prices, stock, discounts, reviews, sales, commissions, integrations, or approvals.
- Prefer official APIs, feeds, authorized integrations, and approved structured supplier data.
- Before checkout, verify item, variant, stock, destination, shipping, landed economics, and checkout eligibility when relevant.
- Historical counts are context only; current sellability requires live verification.

## Supplier Acceptance
Require:
- real catalog/feed/API or approved structured data
- stock and variants
- pricing
- approved media rights
- shipping cost/ETA and destination eligibility
- tracking
- returns/warranty
- payment timing
- authenticity/quality review

CJ has been a primary connected lane; verify current quotes/stock/shipping live.
EPROLO has been evaluated; verify present connection state before claiming it is connected.
Printful/Gooten were evaluated as zero-inventory print-on-demand options; current status must be re-verified.
eBay was explicitly removed and should not be reintroduced unless the owner revisits that decision.

## Checkout
Historical state reached PRELAUNCH with payment-session/ledger work and dry-run testing. Real charging remained gated pending callbacks, order creation, supplier dispatch, tracking, and full E2E readiness. Treat this as history until current state is verified.

## Catalog Direction
The storefront should feel genuinely full before promotion, using real current products and strong imagery, with sizes/colors/variants, shipping and availability truth.

Core assortment direction includes:
- women first
- men
- accessories/jewelry
- sports/gym
- beachwear
- lingerie
- kids/baby
- tech and phone accessories
- pets
- beauty/fragrance
- toys
- home/office lighting
- luggage/travel/office/gifts

## UX Direction
- Clean Shein/Temu-like browse experience
- White default, optional dark mode
- Fast product grid
- Working like/save
- Clear prices
- Useful product media
- Load-more/infinite browse behavior
- Variants and size charts
- stock/availability
- order tracking
- personalized suggestions

## Personalization
HUNT Deal Builder can detect complementary items around a primary product and construct verified-value baskets.
Any coupon, discount, reward, or free-item threshold must be economically real and explainable.

## Merchant Program
Shops/brands may publish through a controlled merchant/API program with seller tracking, listing rules, economics, and truth/quality gates.

## Country Truth
Country experiences must reflect actual:
- currency
- destination shipping and ETA
- taxes/VAT
- returns
- profitable eligible products

## Marketing
Start organic/free first.
Paid media waits until checkout, attribution, fulfillment, pricing, and profitability are verified.
Optimize for Net Contribution rather than GMV/ROAS alone.
Track Product Open → Save → Cart → Purchase and AI/referral acquisition where available.
