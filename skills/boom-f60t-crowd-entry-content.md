## F60T-30 — Platform Content Router

Translate one commercial opportunity differently per platform.

Examples:

Google → answer + product truth
TikTok → hook + demonstration
Pinterest → visual collection
Reddit → useful answer/comparison
YouTube → explanation/comparison/demo
Instagram → visual/lifestyle/reel
AI agent → structured product truth

Do not mass-copy identical creative across platforms.
