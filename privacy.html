1. What this notice covers

This notice explains how HUNT DEAL handles personal information when you browse, create an account, save preferences, use checkout, contact support or interact with security and analytics features.

2. Information we may collect

  • Account information such as email, account identifier and supported sign-in provider.
  • Checkout and shipping information such as name, contact details, address and destination.
  • Cart, saved items, preferences and survey choices you intentionally provide.
  • Support messages, return requests and order-related communications.
  • Technical, security and usage events such as device/browser information, page events, timestamps and anti-abuse signals.
  • Payment evidence and transaction identifiers after payment is activated; HUNT does not ask you to enter card details directly on the current pre-launch checkout page.

3. Why we use information

  • Provide and secure the service.
  • Verify products, shipping, checkout and orders.
  • Remember user-selected preferences and saved items.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, duplicate transactions and unauthorized access.
  • Provide support, returns, refunds and delivery updates.
  • Measure reliability and improve HUNT, subject to applicable consent requirements.
  • Comply with legal, accounting and regulatory duties.

4. Service providers and transfers

HUNT may use hosting, authentication, analytics, payment, logistics, communications and security providers to perform services on its behalf. Information is shared only as reasonably necessary for the relevant function and subject to applicable privacy and security requirements. Some providers may process data in other countries; HUNT will use the safeguards required by applicable law.

5. Cookies and local storage

HUNT may use cookies or browser storage for essential functions, account/session continuity, saved preferences, cart state, security and measurement. Optional analytics or marketing technologies will be used only in accordance with the consent rules applicable to the user and market. Browser controls can remove or block storage, although some features may then stop working.

6. Retention and security

HUNT retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, security, dispute handling, accounting, legal obligations and fraud prevention. Access controls, server-side authorization and other technical safeguards are used to reduce unauthorized access; no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to personal information about you and to request correction, deletion or other handling where the law provides. Requests can be sent to hacoachle@gmail.com. We may request reasonable information to verify identity before acting on a request.

8. Children

HUNT is a general shopping service and is not designed to intentionally collect sensitive information from children. Where law requires parental or guardian authorization for a particular processing activity, HUNT will require the appropriate authorization before that activity is enabled.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this notice when HUNT features, providers or legal requirements change. The latest update date appears above. Privacy contact: hacoachle@gmail.com.

These policies are intended to operate alongside mandatory consumer and privacy law. Where applicable law grants stronger rights, those rights prevail. Before real-money checkout is enabled, HUNT will publish the additional transaction disclosures required for the active market, including the registered business address where required.
