How HUNT handles data.

This notice describes the current HUNT storefront and owner systems. Mandatory privacy rights in the user's jurisdiction always prevail.

Data we may process

  • Account and authentication data when you sign in.
  • Session, page, category, search, product-view, save/like, cart and checkout analytics used to operate and improve HUNT.
  • Shopping preferences, destination market, selected variants and cart state.
  • Order, shipping and payment-session metadata if real-money checkout is later activated.
  • Merchant/seller onboarding data for approved marketplace participants.

HUNT's current checkout does not collect card details on the storefront page. Payment details must be handled by an authorized payment provider when payment is activated.

Why we use data

  • Provide account, catalog, search, recommendation, cart and checkout functions.
  • Recheck product, stock, shipping, pricing and fraud/safety signals.
  • Measure funnel performance and improve relevance, reliability and accessibility.
  • Meet legal, accounting, security, consumer-protection and dispute obligations.

Personalization must not rely on sensitive traits for advertising or promotion decisions.

Storage, security and retention

HUNT uses technical and organizational controls, including access restrictions and row-level database controls, to protect personal data. Data should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, legal obligations, security and dispute handling. Specific production retention periods must be finalized before real-money launch.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, receive a copy of certain data, or complain to a supervisory authority. Requests can be sent to . Local mandatory law may provide additional rights.

International processing

HUNT may use service providers in different countries for hosting, analytics, payments, supplier fulfillment and support. Where law requires safeguards for international transfers, the production service must implement them before processing covered data.

Cookies and local storage

HUNT may use browser storage for cart state, destination market, interface settings, authentication state and similar essential functions. Non-essential tracking or advertising technologies must be separately assessed for consent requirements in the visitor's jurisdiction before they are enabled.
