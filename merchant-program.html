HUNT MERCHANT PROGRAM · PILOT V1

Clear rules before
anything goes live.

This page summarizes HUNT's current pilot operating rules. It is not a substitute for the final signed merchant agreement or market-specific legal review.

Commercial model

Initial listing fee: $0. Planning commission: default 12%, adjustable by approved store/category within a 10–15% planning band. Sponsored placement is a separate reviewed product and is never auto-launched or auto-billed.

Seller of Record & payouts

The merchant remains Seller of Record by default unless a separate signed agreement says otherwise. The pilot uses a 14-day payout-hold planning baseline. Payouts remain disabled until KYC, payout configuration and required operational checks pass.

Product truth

Merchants must keep price, inventory, variants, shipping promises and tracking accurate. Products can be paused when freshness checks fail. HUNT does not permit fake discounts, fake reviews, unsupported availability or invented compare-at prices.

Brands & authenticity

Merchants must have the legal right to sell and market every branded product they submit. HUNT may request authorization, authenticity or media-rights evidence before publication.

Safety & restricted products

Restricted, unsafe, counterfeit or otherwise prohibited products are not eligible for HUNT publication. Product safety review is separate from commercial approval.

Returns, shipping & support

Merchants must follow applicable consumer law and HUNT-approved returns rules for the markets in which they sell. Shipping SLA, tracking, cancellation, refund and defective-product handling must be operational before launch.

Activation path

  1. HUNT owner activates a Merchant Program version.
  2. Merchant accepts the current program acknowledgements.
  3. KYC and payout setup are reviewed.
  4. Store is approved.
  5. Products are submitted and safety-reviewed.
  6. Freshness and BOOM NET ranking determine discovery eligibility.
  7. Checkout or sponsored activity activates only after its separate approval gates pass.
